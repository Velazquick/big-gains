Limited usage and technical information

Big Gains collects limited first-party information to understand product usage and reliability: authenticated app opens, workout start and completion events, Program activation, exercise Swap use, recovery prompts and actions, and application error categories. Events include app release, coarse device/browser type, and whether the app is displayed in a browser or standalone window.

Training weights, repetitions, bodyweight, exercise names, private workout notes, raw error messages and stacks are not included in product telemetry. We do not use session replay, advertising identifiers or device fingerprinting.

Events are associated with your signed-in account and selected profile. The ownerâ€™s read-only Operator Console can see account identity, confirmation status, aggregate workout counts and dates, Program adoption, and these limited operational observations. It does not expose complete workouts or training contents.

Telemetry is best-effort: it can be dropped when offline or unavailable and is never required to train. Raw events are excluded from reports after 90 days and removed by daily cleanup, normally within one additional day. Physically deleting the associated account, profile or Auth database record deletes its events. An administrative soft-delete does not immediately remove those events; they age out through cleanup. First observed app-open date is retained until account deletion to calculate return cohorts. Existing product storage and backup behavior is separate from this telemetry policy.
